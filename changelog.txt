v2.8.0
- Music OSD: a chapters button, shown while something with chapters is
  playing. It opens Kodi's chapter list, which until now was reachable only
  during video playback. Create bookmark and Clear bookmarks are hidden
  there for audio — they write to Kodi's video database, which means nothing
  for an audiobook
- Music OSD: moving right from the transport controls no longer skips the
  first button of the right-hand group
- Home: shorten the TMDB search button to "Search TMDB"
- Removed the "Hide play speed OSD on tempo change" setting. It suppressed
  the seek bar while an add-on nudged Kodi's play speed to stay in sync;
  inputstream.tempo handles tempo itself now and no longer touches play
  speed, so there was nothing left to suppress

Note for AudioBookShelf users: chapter seeking through an add-on that
supplies its own stream is broken in Kodi 22.0 beta 1 itself and jumps to
the start of the book. Kodi builds since mid-July have the fix. Chapters in
ordinary files are unaffected.

v2.6.0
• Home: add per-library Movies and Shows sections from Kofin, created in Skin settings → Main menu items → Jellyfin library sections. Each section clones the stock stack with paths scoped to that library (synced Kodi nodes or live plugin browse). Stock Movies/Shows stay; the existing widget toggles apply to every section of that type
• Search: a "Search Kofin" entry in the search dialog, opening the Kofin add-on's own search of your Jellyfin server. Shown whenever Kofin is installed. It finds what the local library search cannot — a Jellyfin library you browse rather than sync has no Kodi rows to search
• Movies and TV shows home screens: the Embuary Info buttons now follow that add-on's own settings, so In cinemas, In cinemas soon and Next aired can each be switched off in Embuary Info's Skin settings rather than by editing the skin. Adds a "Search The Movie DB" button beside them

[B]2.4.0[/B]
• Music home screen: a "Recent queues" button on the categories row, opening the Restore Music Queue add-on's pop-up in place. Shown whenever that add-on is enabled. The add-on used to edit the skin's own files to put it there, and a skin update took it away again
